Documentation & Scoring Rules

This system calculates cumulative penalty scores ranging from 0 to 100 based on automated heuristics, keyword pattern checks, and URL inspections.

0 pts (Safe): No indicators or threat matches found.
1 - 40 pts (Low Risk): Mild keyword triggers or minor anomalies.
41 - 75 pts (Medium Risk): Multiple social engineering indicators or shortener links.
76 - 100 pts (Critical): Severe header spoofing or raw IP exploit links.
